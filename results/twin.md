## Conditional tail scenarios (both bonds exhausted)

Largest error of a driver share read from the library: 0.076 (binomial standard deviation at n = 200: 0.035). ## Budget allocation

25 certified queries (5 targets x 5 scenarios), budget 2,000,000 quantum queries, weights 4 for tail targets (exceedance, stop-loss, exhaustion), 1 otherwise. Error model: relative error = kappa / n with the measured IQAE constant 20.6. Optimal allocation lowers the weighted squared error by 93% against an equal split and by 46% against a split proportional to difficulty (shares from 0.6% to 25.1% of the budget).
